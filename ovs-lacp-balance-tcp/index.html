Model assumptions

  • br-ex, br-int and the patch ports are collapsed into one "local" port on br-phy, the bridge that owns the bond. Bond: bond_mode=balance-tcp lacp=active, defaults otherwise (lacp-time=slow, lacp-fallback-ab=false).
  • MLAG mode: sw1/sw2 present one LACP system-ID, so each node's four links form one port-channel (Po-A, Po-B) that spans both switches, with one synchronised MAC table. Independent mode: two system-IDs; OVS attaches only the aggregator of its lead member (eno1 → sw1), so eno3/eno4 are out of sync and sw2 keeps them out of its bundle.
  • Flows are hashed on L2/L3/L4 into 256 buckets; which member a given flow uses (vm-web → eno2 and eno3, pods across all four) is illustrative. The switch hashes return traffic independently.
  • A LAG is one logical port to the switch: floods go once per port-channel, never back onto the source port-channel.
  • The pods square stands for all Kubernetes pod-network traffic on the node (node MAC via br-ex); unlike SLB, its many flows are spread across members.
  • Timeline is compressed: animation time is not wall-clock. The clock shows real elapsed time.
