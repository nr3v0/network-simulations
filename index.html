Model assumptions

  • br-ex, br-int and the patch ports are collapsed into one "local" port on br-phy, the bridge that owns the SLB bond in the Red Hat topology.
  • sw1/sw2 are drawn with one MLAG-synchronised MAC table. All 8 host-facing ports are individual VLAN trunks (not an MLAG port-channel), so floods reach every member.
  • Hash bucket assignments (vm-web → eno2 on Node A, eno3 on Node B) are illustrative; the real bucket is hash(src MAC, VLAN) into 256 entries and is assigned arbitrarily on first use.
  • Active member is eno1 on both nodes (first member enabled). OVS only names it deterministically "by accident"; check with ovs-appctl bond/show.
  • Timeline is compressed: animation time is not wall-clock. The clock shows real elapsed time.
