Network simulations

Step-through, RaftScope-style animations of how Open vSwitch bonds learn and re-learn MAC addresses across four ports and two switches — during cold start, VM live migration, hash rebalancing and failures. Built to explain the behaviour (and the risks) to platform and network teams.

SLB Scope balance-slb

Source-MAC load balancing with no switch cooperation. Shows the active-member broadcast rule, reflected-broadcast drops, the gratuitous-ARP exception and 5 s lock, and why a RARP-only migration announcement can strand a VM.

  • Cold start → migration, lifecycle with N rebalances
  • All five SLB complications from the OVS bonding docs
  • MLAG peer-link vs. independent switches

LACP Scope balance-tcp

802.3ad with per-flow hashing. Shows why a LAG makes migration a single MAC move, why rebalances become invisible, and the LACP-specific failure modes: un-negotiated day 0, MLAG peer-link loss, slow timers, elephant flows.

  • Same topology and scenarios for side-by-side comparison
  • LACP member states and partner-mismatch handling
  • MLAG (one LACP system) vs. two independent systems
